# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Wardrobe: a wooden button in the corner of the pond opens a menu for dressing up the frog and changing its pond.
- Outfits: four hats (mushroom hat, golden crown, wizard hat, baseball cap) and two kinds of glasses (round glasses, sunglasses). One of each at a time. They stay on through hopping, eating and celebrating.
- Pond themes: Classic, Moonlight, Sakura and Autumn. Switch any time without reloading.
- Decorations: flower patch, lantern, lily pad flower, shore rocks and mushrooms, each in its own spot. They sit behind the frog and bugs, so bugs stay easy to see.
- Whispers: every 10 minutes or so the frog says something encouraging in a small speech bubble. Only time with the pond on screen counts, and it waits until the frog isn't busy eating. New settings `froggit.whispers.enabled` and `froggit.whispers.interval` (5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes).
- The XP bar is now a wooden plank to match the rest of the pond. On narrow sidebars it hides the numbers, which are still shown on hover.
- Your outfit, pond and decorations are saved, sync with Settings Sync, and are kept when you reset progress.

## 0.1.1

- Marketplace page: demo animation, badges, and links to the GitHub repository.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Pixel-art frog pond in the Explorer sidebar.
- Frog idles, blinks, hops around and turns on its own.
- Errors appear as beetles and warnings as gnats, with a capped swarm size.
- Fixing a problem makes the frog chase and eat a bug; a clean workspace makes it celebrate.
- XP for fixes, clean code, meaningful saves and coding sessions, with anti-spam limits.
- Egg → Baby Frog growth, with a hatching animation. Progress persists and syncs.
- Settings: count warnings, reduced motion.
